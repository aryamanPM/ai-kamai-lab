# YouTube Research Engine

The research engine evaluates niches and individual video opportunities before production.

## Current leading hypothesis

**Practical AI + business + productivity for Indian viewers**, with a strong focus on real demonstrations and experiments rather than generic AI news.

This is a hypothesis, not a permanent niche decision.

## Evidence

- YouTube reported that more than 200 million logged-in users in India had shopping-related searches and shopping watch time grew more than 250% year over year. This supports commercially useful, product-led content. See Google's October 2025 YouTube Shopping announcement.
- YouTube expanded Shopping affiliate access in March 2026 to eligible YPP creators with 500+ subscribers, improving the monetization path for smaller channels.
- Google says YouTube and Google are present in 93% of Indian consumer journeys for discovering a new brand, product or retailer.
- Indian government and YouTube launched a national AI skilling initiative in March 2026, reinforcing AI education as a strategic growth area.

## Research caution

Third-party RPM and growth figures are directional, not guaranteed. The system will learn from actual channel analytics once publishing begins.
