# AI Kamai Lab — YouTube Automation OS

AI-assisted, YouTube-first content operating system for an India-focused channel.

## Mission

Build an original, useful media brand that compounds views, watch time, subscribers, community and revenue. AI is used as a production tool; it is not a substitute for originality or editorial judgment.

## Platform

YouTube: long-form + Shorts + community + future live content.

## Research Direction

Initial research indicates strong opportunity around practical, high-intent knowledge content for India, especially AI-enabled work/business/productivity and creator-economy topics. The final niche is selected by the research/scoring engine rather than assumed permanently.

## Pipeline

Research → Opportunity Scoring → Channel Strategy → Topic Selection → Script → Fact Check → Voice/Audio → Video Production → Thumbnail/Title → QA → Publish → Analytics → Learning → Research

## Growth Model

Long-form builds watch time, authority and revenue depth. Shorts provide discovery and funnel qualified viewers into related long-form videos. Performance decisions are based on retention, click-through rate, engaged views, returning viewers, subscribers per view and revenue signals—not raw views alone.

## Monetization

YPP ads and Premium revenue, YouTube Shopping/affiliate opportunities where eligible, fan funding, brand partnerships, and eventually owned products/services.

## AI / Policy

Generated or meaningfully altered realistic content must be disclosed where YouTube requires it. The system rejects copied/reused low-value content, fake engagement, spam and copyright-infringing assets.

## Free-first Infrastructure

Prefer GitHub, GitHub Actions, open-source utilities and free tiers. Paid infrastructure is introduced only when a free option is technically inadequate.

## Status

Instagram project cancelled. YouTube conversion in progress.
